The Challenge

One week. One brief. No shortcuts.

Pure Mountain Fun was a sponsored design sprint that challenged teams to develop a complete product and experience concept covering research, ideation, prototyping, and presentation, all in seven days. That kind of timeline doesn't leave much room to second-guess. You make a call and you build from it.

Context

Speed as a design constraint.

With only one week to research, ideate, prototype, and present, the project focused heavily on rapid collaboration and clear communication. The challenge pushed our team to make decisions quickly while still developing a concept that felt cohesive, engaging, and visually compelling.

Process

How the week unfolded.

  • Collaborative ideation — open sessions to surface every direction fast
  • Rapid concept sketching — dozens of directions roughed in the first two days
  • Team alignment — converging on a single strong concept with clear rationale
  • Prototype development — physical and digital models to pressure-test the idea
  • Presentation prep — tight visual narrative built for competition under real time pressure
Ideation

Quantity first. Quality followed.

The team committed to a generate-first approach: sketch fast, explore broadly, and make deliberate cuts once the strongest directions emerged. Speed didn't compromise quality; it clarified it.

Design Direction

Modular. Tactile. Outdoor-ready.

The concept centered on a modular product system designed for outdoor play and physical interaction. Every decision about form, material, and assembly logic was driven by the experience of use in motion, in the mountains, with other people. Tactile, playful, and deliberately incomplete on its own.

Outcome Competition Result

Out of 200+ teams.
We placed second.

The project was awarded 2nd place for its combination of strong visual identity, collaborative execution, and an engaging product experience that felt genuinely connected to the brief. This project reinforced how quickly strong ideas can develop when collaboration, rapid iteration, and clear storytelling come together.
