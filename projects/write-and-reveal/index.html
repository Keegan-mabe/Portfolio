Write and Reveal Wand

2025 Kids toy 6 week project
Project brief

For this project, I was challenged to design a product that used an atomizer in a new way. Instead of creating another basic misting product, I wanted to see how the technology could be used to make learning more fun and interactive for kids. From there, I started exploring different ways mist could become part of play, reading, and writing.

Research
Ideation
Prototyping
My solution

The final concept became the Write and Reveal Wand, an interactive toy that pairs a misting wand with a clear wax pen. Kids can draw or write something that is almost invisible, then use the wand to reveal it through fog. Through testing with kids and building multiple prototypes, I refined the form to work better for smaller hands and created an experience that mixes learning with a little bit of magic.
