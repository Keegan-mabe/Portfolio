Stark
Watch

Wearable Interface Concept for the Stark Future VARG

Designed as an alternative to traditional mounted dashboards, the Stark Watch explores how rider telemetry, navigation, and bike information could move to a dedicated wearable experience built specifically for off-road riding.

BUILT

THE RIDER.

Concept

The idea came from noticing riders remove their phones from the bike mount entirely before riding, leaving them without easy access to live ride data. Current setups depend heavily on mobile devices, but phones are not purpose-built for aggressive off-road use. They can feel fragile, distracting, and disconnected from the machine.

The Stark Watch explores a dedicated wearable interface that keeps essential ride information accessible both on and off the bike. Speed, battery state, ride mode, and navigation cues — designed to be glanceable, durable, and built around the VARG ecosystem from the start.

Design Thinking

What It Explores

01

Speed + Battery Monitoring

Real-time readout of velocity and charge state designed to be read in motion, at a glance.

02

Ride Mode Switching

Access and adjust ride modes from the wrist without reaching for the bike interface mid-session.

03

Navigation Cues

Simplified directional prompts for trail navigation without the distraction of a full map view.

04

Bike Telemetry

Key performance data from the VARG system surfaced in a form factor that stays with the rider.

05

Haptic Alerts

Critical notifications delivered through vibration so riders stay informed without looking down.

06

Weather-Resistant Build

Built for the conditions of off-road riding. Mud, dust, and water are part of the environment.

07

Glove-Friendly Interaction

Inputs sized and positioned for use with riding gloves on, not just bare hands in a clean room.

08

VARG Ecosystem Integration

Designed to feel like a natural extension of the Stark Future product family, not a third-party add-on.
