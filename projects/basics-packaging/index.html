Basics

Packaging Design  /  Branding  /  Research

Basics explores how discreet branding and educational packaging can reduce embarrassment and discomfort surrounding hygiene and sexual wellness products for teens and young adults.

Category Packaging Design
Branding / Research
Focus Discreet Educational
Systems
Timeline 4 Weeks
Overview

Design that disappears.

Basics focused on redesigning the experience around personal care and sexual wellness products that many young adults feel uncomfortable purchasing or talking about. Condoms, pads, tampons, razors. Products that are completely normal parts of everyday life, but whose retail presence often makes them feel anything but.

Existing packaging in this category is often loud, highly gendered, and visually attention-grabbing. That visual noise adds unnecessary social anxiety around already sensitive experiences. Basics rethinks this through minimal branding, discreet packaging systems, and approachable educational design.

Problem

Loud design in a quiet moment.

The personal care aisle is one of the loudest in retail — bright colors, bold claims, gendered imagery. For a teenager already navigating uncertainty around their body and health, this environment compounds the discomfort rather than easing it.

Process

From insight to system.

Research framed the problem. Iteration shaped the solution, working through brand language, typographic systems, structural packaging forms, and educational hierarchy before arriving at a unified, restrained identity.

Research

What the data said.

  • Teens often avoid asking questions about sexual wellness and hygiene due to embarrassment
  • Current packaging relies heavily on bright colors and highly gendered branding
  • Purchasing these products can create acute anxiety in public retail environments
  • Educational information is often inaccessible, overwhelming, or culturally stigmatized
  • Discreet packaging can meaningfully normalize these everyday experiences
Final Outcome

Quiet by design.

Rather than making these products louder, Basics focuses on making users feel more comfortable. The system prioritizes privacy, clarity, and accessible education while maintaining a clean and modern visual identity.

IF Design Award

Submitted for recognition.

Basics was submitted to the iF Design Award for its approach to inclusive design, emotional discretion, and educational packaging. A project that started with a real problem and ended with a system that genuinely tries to solve it.

Designed for
comfort.

This project pushed me to think beyond aesthetics and focus on emotional experience. Designing for sensitive topics required balancing discretion, accessibility, education, and visual identity in a way that felt approachable rather than clinical.
