Basics Packaging

2026 Packaging and branding 4 week project
Overview

Condoms, pads, tampons, razors: everyday products sold in the loudest aisle in retail, in bright, gendered packaging that adds anxiety to an already sensitive purchase. Basics answers with discreet packaging and education built into the box, for teens and young adults.

Process
Research
  • Teens avoid asking about sexual wellness and hygiene out of embarrassment.
  • Current packaging leans on bright colours and gendered branding.
  • Buying these products in public can cause real anxiety.
  • Education is often hard to reach, overwhelming or stigmatized.
  • Discreet packaging can help normalize everyday experiences.
Outcome

Rather than making these products louder, Basics makes the people buying them more comfortable: private, clear, and quietly educational.

iF Design Award

Submitted to the iF Design Award for its approach to inclusive design, emotional discretion and educational packaging.

Reflection

Designing for a sensitive subject meant looking past aesthetics to emotional experience: balancing discretion, access, education and identity so the result feels approachable, not clinical.
